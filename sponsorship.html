About the Event

Our Community, Our Celebration

Sattva Park Cubix is a vibrant and growing residential community in North Bangalore, with families having moved in since 2023. As the community grows stronger, we are proud to host our first-ever Onam celebration — bringing together families from across the community for a day of festivities: Pookalam competition, traditional Sadya, cultural performances, games, and more.

This is your brand's chance to be part of a landmark community moment and connect with an engaged, growing, and concentrated audience in a warm, celebratory setting.

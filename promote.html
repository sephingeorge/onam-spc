Separate Offering

🪧 Flex Display

Want your brand's own standee displayed at the event? Bring your fully printed flex banner or standee — we place it at a spot close to the celebration area. No printing cost on our end.

What you get
  • Your own branded standee / flex banner displayed at the event
  • Placed at a visible spot near the celebration area
  • You provide the flex — fully printed and ready to display
Good to know
  • Final placement decided by the event team based on space availability
  • Maximum one standee per brand unless multiple slots are booked
  • No competing brand standees placed adjacent to each other
